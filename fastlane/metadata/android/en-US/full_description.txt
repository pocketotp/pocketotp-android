Your assistant cannot see the verification code that just arrived on your phone. Renting a number, or handing over your whole inbox, is a poor fix.

PocketOTP runs on your own Android phone and forwards new SMS verification codes to your own AI assistant. It is for your own numbers only — not a number pool, and not for reselling codes.

What it does

• Watches incoming SMS on every SIM and extracts 4–8 digit codes
• Uploads matching messages to a relay you pair with (QR code or manual token)
• Stays connected in the background so new codes can reach your assistant promptly
• Sends a text from a chosen SIM only after you long-press Allow on the phone
• Blocks bank and payment-related messages by default (the keyword list is editable)

What it does not do

• It does not read your historical inbox
• It does not upload ordinary conversations
• It does not send SMS without your confirmation on the device

The Android app is Apache-2.0. Official builds are signed APKs on GitHub Releases (Google Play does not grant these SMS permissions to ordinary apps). The hosted relay at pocketotp.com is a separate, closed-source service. Requires Android 8.0 or later. Dual-SIM phones are supported.

Permissions

• RECEIVE_SMS, READ_SMS — receive new texts. The app does not scan the inbox; READ_SMS is required on some devices
• SEND_SMS — send a text after you confirm
• READ_PHONE_STATE, READ_PHONE_NUMBERS — tell which SIM received a message or should send one
• INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — talk to the relay over HTTPS
• POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT — persistent notification, connection, and send-confirmation alerts
• RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — restart after reboot if forwarding is enabled
• WAKE_LOCK — keep the connection from sleeping
• REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS — optional one-tap exemption from the setup checklist
• SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW — optional; lets the confirm screen open from the background on some phones
• CAMERA — optional; only used by the QR pairing scanner
